# SUDARI 2.0.1 🦦🫧

Keep your little otter, its notes and its shell together.

- Pinned notes now sit beside the pet's dialogue and timer instead of at the top of the transparent window.
- Notes, speech and the timer editor share one measured layout with 8px gaps. Long text remains scrollable; the smallest pet size has extra room for the editor's controls.
- Compact focus/break and round badges fit their text. Longer translations wrap within the shell without covering the time or round count.
- Settings explain what the pinned note does and how to hide it, in all 12 languages. Japanese now consistently says river otter (カワウソ).
- Added native Electron regression coverage for simultaneous overlays, all 17 poses and all 12 languages, plus compact timer labels in both phases.

## Downloads

- **Windows:** `Sudari-2.0.1-Setup.exe` or `Sudari-2.0.1-Portable.exe`.
- **Mac M series:** `Sudari-2.0.1-mac-arm64.dmg` (ZIP also available).
- **Mac Intel:** `Sudari-2.0.1-mac-x64.dmg` (ZIP also available).

Windows 10/11 x64; macOS 12+. Builds are unsigned and not Apple-notarized. macOS keyboard/scroll reactions require Accessibility permission. SHA-256 checksums are attached.
